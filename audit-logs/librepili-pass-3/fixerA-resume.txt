8. **Cookie login:** keys and values are trimmed and values keep their `=` characters.
9. **WBI keys:**

10. **Error toasts:** they show only scheme, host and path.
11. **Redirects to another host:** the cookie, authorization and account headers and the old query parameters are dropped, and the account binding is set to `NoAccount`.
